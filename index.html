Clinical Background

Precision Neuro-Electrophysiology & Patient-Centric Care

I am Ganesh Chandra Jena, a dedicated Neuro Medical Technologist practicing in Bargarh, Odisha. At Neuro Polyclinic, I personally perform specialized neuro-diagnostic procedures for pediatric, adolescent, and adult patients.

My clinical methodology emphasizes exact anatomical surface electrode placement, low skin-electrode impedance, rigorous 50Hz notch and physiological artifact elimination, and detailed waveform measurement to provide neurologists and referring physicians with dependable diagnostic data.

✓ Anatomical Nerve Localization ✓ 10-20 International EEG System ✓ Pediatric & Adult Protocols

10-20 EEG Montage

Accurate scalp measurement, symmetrical electrode positioning, and generalized or focal epileptiform discharge verification.

50Hz Artifact Filtering

Optimal notch filter tuning, gain calibration, and sweep speed control on Allengers and RMS neuro-diagnostic machines.

NCS Lead Precision

Dedicated active, reference, and ground (G1/G2/GND) NCS lead placement with supramaximal stimulation for accurate CMAP & SNAP traces.

Clinical Reporting

Comprehensive distal latency, amplitude, and conduction velocity (NCV) tables paired with structured clinical impressions.

Diagnostic Portfolio

Neuro-Diagnostic Investigations

Comprehensive electrodiagnostic procedures conducted personally at Neuro Polyclinic using specialized NCS leads and multi-channel digital EEG/EP recording systems.

01 / NCV

Nerve Conduction Studies (NCS)

Evaluation of peripheral motor and sensory nerves (Median, Ulnar, Radial, Common Peroneal, Tibial, and Sural nerves) to assess peripheral neuropathy, carpal tunnel syndrome, and axonal or demyelinating lesions.

MNCS & SNCS CMAP / SNAP
02 / LATE

F-Wave & H-Reflex Studies

Proximal nerve root and spinal cord segment conduction testing for cervical and lumbosacral radiculopathies, Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS), and early polyneuropathy evaluation.

F-Min Latency Tibial H-Reflex
03 / EEG

Electroencephalography (EEG)

Awake and sleep digital EEG recordings for pediatric, adolescent, and adult patients with standard activation procedures (Hyperventilation & Photic Stimulation) and epileptiform waveform analysis.

10-20 Montage Pediatric & Adult
04 / RNST

Repetitive Nerve Stimulation (RNST)

Specialized low-frequency (3Hz) train stimulation protocols on distal and proximal muscles to evaluate decremental CMAP responses in neuromuscular junction disorders such as Myasthenia Gravis.

3Hz Train Decrement Study
05 / VEP

Visual Evoked Potentials (VEP)

Pattern-reversal and flash VEP testing to measure P100 wave latency and amplitude for evaluating optic neuritis, demyelinating disease, and anterior visual pathway integrity.

P100 Latency Optic Pathway
06 / BERA

Brainstem Auditory Evoked Response (BERA)

Acoustic click-evoked auditory brainstem testing analyzing Waves I–V absolute and inter-peak latencies for pediatric hearing threshold estimation and retrocochlear pathway evaluation.

Waves I–V BAEP / BERA
